## Consensual sex

I see consenting to becoming pregnant, or at least implicitly accepting the risks of pregnancy by engaging in reckless sex, then waiting till the fetus becomes sentient, should disqualify one from aborting that sentient fetus if the pregnancy doesn't pose a significant health risk to the mother.
→ [[Fetal sentience]]

## Rape

Rape cases are different and I think the violinist analogy applies as they're basically having a sentient being forced to rely on them.
→ [[Abortion Ethics]]

## Incest

In many cases I think it's ok in incest cases too as it commonly involves highly unbalanced power dynamics that undermine consent.
