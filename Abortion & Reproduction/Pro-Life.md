> Rebuttals to common pro-life claims: post-abortion syndrome isn't established, conception is a multi-hour process rather than a moment, not every fertilization yields an embryo, and pregnancy itself carries substantial maternal risk.

## Post-abortion syndrome

Post-abortion syndrome (PAS) isn't real. There are women who do feel genuine distress or regret after an abortion... as with many hard life choices. But calling it a syndrome gives it prevalence it doesn't have.
[Dadlez 2009](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/abs/10.1111/j.1467-8519.2009.01739.x?casa_token=1WRNrcY3LicAAAAA%3A9OVJsOw-sxHavkPLyDgcqnOm0Dpq_V166kgWPpaX1X-d18xXnvU79WZzIgXr3SsloElOaAUOlIY0-2Ig)

## Peri-implantation sex

For those who believe the right to life begins at conception, is it immoral to have sex during the peri-implantation period of a woman's menstrual cycle (there's evidence it reduces the chance of implantation of a zygote and thus increases chance of miscarriage)?
![[Peri-implantation intercourse fecundability table.png|360]]
having sex more often during the peri-implantation period was associated with having sex more often during the fertile period. So this effect is unlikely to be due to a decrease in intercourse during fertile period.

## Conception is a process

Fertilization is a multi-hour process, so conception is not a "moment". We have sperm entry, pronuclear migration, and the actual fusion of the two pronuclei happen over many hours. PLK-1 is needed for that pronuclear fusion part, which shows there are discrete molecular gates before a single unified genome even exists.
[Ma 2020](https://rupress.org/jcb/article/219/2/e202001048/133643/Membrane-fusion-drives-pronuclear-meeting-in-the)  
[NCBI Gene: PLK1](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/gene/5347)

## Hydatidiform moles

Not every fertilization yields an embryo. A hydatidiform mole is a fertilized product that develops into placental overgrowth with no viable embryo. Complete moles are usually androgenetic, while recurrent biparental moles have one maternal and one paternal set but fail to lay down proper maternal imprints. So they behave as all-paternal 
[Van den Veyver 2006](https://academic.oup.com/humupd/article/12/3/233/554310)

Mole cancer risk to mother exists but is easily treatable. Around 15% transformation to choriocarcinoma in complete moles (around 2-4% in Western countries), 0.5% in partial moles, and unknown in biparental. But over a 90% cure rate even if it happens, and around 100% if monitored and treated by stage II. 
[Goldstein 2012](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22244665/)

## Maternal risks of pregnancy

Pregnancy itself can carry a large maternal risk. Gestational diabetes 2-9%, pre-eclampsia 2-8%, eclampsia 1.4%, HELLP 0.5-0.9%, VTE around 2/1000 deliveries, placental abruption 0.4-3.8% by region. Importantly, 31% of women report long-term health problems persisting past six months postpartum.
[Haram 2009](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/labs/pmc/articles/PMC2654858) (HELLP)  
[Downes 2017](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/labs/pmc/articles/PMC5683164/) (abruption)  
[Borders 2006](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/abs/10.1016/j.jmwh.2005.10.014) (long-term problems)

Multiple-gestation pregnancies increase maternal risk, with a dose-response from twins to triplets and quadruplets.
[Luke 2008](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/18177828/)  
[Wen 2004](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/15295375/)
