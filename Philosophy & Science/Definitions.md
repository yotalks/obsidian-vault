Definitions aren't propositions, they don't have a truth value. A definition can be incoherent, but not true or false.

Like to define pi as the ratio of the circumference to the diameter of a circle, doesn't entail a claim of any kind, it is simply a construct for it's usage. Individuals are simply agreeing to use this Greek letter to substitute the given notion.

 A proposition on the other hand, attempts to add new information (by predication, etc). Like if I said pi multiplied by 2 times the radius of this circle is 25 inches. This is a proposition because it can have a truth value.

Definitions at the end of the day are social constructs, which isn't to belittle them, they can be very valuable, it's just to show there is not truth or falsity to any given definition.
