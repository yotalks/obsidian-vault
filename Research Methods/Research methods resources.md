Free courses, tools and readings for research methodology, epidemiology and biostatistics.

## Courses

Boston University's public health learning modules give a good overview of research methodology, biostatistics and related topics.
- [All modules](https://sphweb.bumc.bu.edu/otlt/MPH-Modules/Menu/index.html)
- [Descriptive studies](https://sphweb.bumc.bu.edu/otlt/MPH-Modules/EP/EP713_DescriptiveEpi/EP713_DescriptiveEpi7.html)
- [Randomized clinical trials](https://sphweb.bumc.bu.edu/otlt/MPH-Modules/EP/EP713_ClinicalTrials/index.html)
- [Case-control studies](https://sphweb.bumc.bu.edu/otlt/MPH-Modules/EP/EP713_Case-Control/index.html)
- [Cohort studies](https://sphweb.bumc.bu.edu/otlt/MPH-Modules/EP/EP713_CohortStudies/index.html)
- [How to read studies and what questions to ask](https://sphweb.bumc.bu.edu/otlt/MPH-Modules/EP/EP713_CriticalReview/index.html)

[Penn State STAT 507: Epidemiological Research Methods](https://online.stat.psu.edu/stat507/)

## Critical appraisal

- [CEBM critical appraisal tools](https://www.cebm.ox.ac.uk/resources/ebm-tools/critical-appraisal-tools): worksheets for judging how reliable, important and applicable a study is.
- [CASP qualitative studies checklist](https://casp-uk.net/casp-tools-checklists/qualitative-studies-checklist/)
- [Critical Appraisal of Clinical Research (2017)](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28658805/)
