## Abbreviations

### 1RM
**1RM / RM** — One-repetition maximum / repetition maximum — the heaviest load you can lift for that many reps.

### AAS
**AAS** — Anabolic-androgenic steroids. See [[Anabolic Androgenic Steroids]].

### BFR
**BFR** — Blood flow restriction (training with a cuff that limits blood flow).

### CNS
**CNS** — Central nervous system (brain and spinal cord). See [[Supraspinal CNS fatigue]].

### CSA
**CSA** — Cross-sectional area (of a muscle or muscle fiber).

### DOMS
**DOMS** — Delayed-onset muscle soreness.

### ECCF
**ECCF** — Excitation-contraction coupling failure — a calcium ion-related fatigue mechanism. See [[Calcium ion-related fatigue]].

### EMAL
**EMAL** — External moment arm length — distance from the load's line of force to the joint. See [[Internal moment arm]].

### IMAL
**IMAL** — Internal moment arm length — distance from a muscle's line of pull to the joint. See [[Internal moment arm]].

### MPS
**MPS** — Muscle protein synthesis.

### MPB
**MPB** — Muscle protein breakdown.

### MyoPS
**MyoPS** — Myofibrillar protein synthesis (the contractile-protein part of MPS).

### MT
**MT** — Mechanical tension. See [[Mechanical tension]].

### CMC
**CMC** — Central motor command — the neural drive the nervous system sends to muscles. See [[Neuromechanical matching]].

### LTR
**LTR** — Length-tension relationship — how much force a muscle fiber can produce at each length. Force peaks on the plateau, where actin and myosin overlap best. See [[Neuromechanical matching]].

### NMM
**NMM** — Neuromechanical matching — neural drive goes to the motor units with the best leverage. See [[Neuromechanical matching]].

### ROM
**ROM** — Range of motion.

### MUR
**MUR** — Motor unit recruitment. See [[Increasing motor unit recruitment]].

### RBE
**RBE** — Repeated bout effect. See [[Repeated bout effect]].

### GLP-1RA
**GLP-1RA** — GLP-1 receptor agonist (semaglutide, tirzepatide, retatrutide…). See [[GLP 1s]].

### GIP
**GIP** — Glucose-dependent insulinotropic polypeptide (also called gastric inhibitory polypeptide); GIPR is its receptor. See [[GLP 1s]].

### MACE
**MACE** — Major adverse cardiovascular events.

### TRT
**TRT** — Testosterone replacement therapy.

### TMAO
**TMAO** — Trimethylamine N-oxide, a gut-microbe metabolite of choline and carnitine. See [[TMAO]].

### LH
**LH / FSH** — Luteinizing hormone / follicle-stimulating hormone — pituitary hormones that drive the menstrual cycle. See [[Menstrual cycle]].

### hCG
**hCG** — Human chorionic gonadotropin, the pregnancy hormone measured in pregnancy tests.

### GHG
**GHG** — Greenhouse gas.

### GWAS
**GWAS / SNP** — Genome-wide association study / single-nucleotide polymorphism.

### SES
**SES** — Socioeconomic status.

### SMD
**SMD** — Standardized mean difference, an effect size used in meta-analysis. See [[Mean difference vs standardized mean difference]].

### NAFLD
**NAFLD** — Non-alcoholic fatty liver disease: excess fat in the liver not caused by alcohol (now also called MASLD). See [[Lipogenic Fructose]].

### DNL
**DNL** — De novo lipogenesis: the liver making new fat from carbohydrate.

### LDL
**LDL** — Low-density lipoprotein (LDL-C: the cholesterol it carries) — the lipoprotein that causes atherosclerosis.

### HDL
**HDL** — High-density lipoprotein (HDL-C: the cholesterol it carries). See [[HDL]].

### CVD
**CVD** — Cardiovascular disease.

### CHD
**CHD** — Coronary heart disease.

### SFA
**SFA** — Saturated fatty acids (saturated fat).

### PUFA
**PUFA** — Polyunsaturated fatty acids (omega-6 and omega-3 fats).

### LA
**LA** — Linoleic acid, the main omega-6 polyunsaturated fat.

### HTN
**HTN** — Hypertension (high blood pressure).

### DM
**DM** — Diabetes mellitus.

### ESRD
**ESRD** — End-stage renal disease (kidney failure).

### DC
**DC** — Dietary cholesterol.

### ApoB
**ApoB** — Apolipoprotein B, one per atherogenic particle (LDL, VLDL, Lp(a)) — the best single lipid predictor of heart disease risk.

### HbA1c
**HbA1c** — Glycated hemoglobin, a measure of average blood sugar over roughly 3 months.

### SCFA
**SCFA** — Short-chain fatty acids (such as butyrate) made when gut bacteria ferment fiber.

### MUFA
**MUFA** — Monounsaturated fatty acids, such as oleic acid in olive oil.

### oxLDL
**oxLDL** — Oxidized LDL, measured in blood with antibody assays such as Mercodia 4E6.

### oxPL
**oxPL** — Oxidized phospholipids, often measured with the E06 antibody and reported per ApoB (oxPL/ApoB).

### TFA
**TFA** — Trans fatty acids (trans fat), mostly from partially hydrogenated oils.

### AA
**AA** — Arachidonic acid, an omega-6 fat the body makes from linoleic acid.

### AMI
**AMI** — Acute myocardial infarction (heart attack).

### ASCVD
**ASCVD** — Atherosclerotic cardiovascular disease: heart attack, stroke and other disease caused by plaque in the arteries. See [[LDL and heart disease]].

### CRP
**CRP** — C-reactive protein, a blood marker of inflammation; hs-CRP is the high-sensitivity test used for cardiovascular risk.

### MR
**MR** — Mendelian randomization: using gene variants that shift an exposure to estimate its causal effect.

### BCC
**BCC** — Basal cell carcinoma, the most common skin cancer.

### SCC
**SCC** — Squamous cell carcinoma of the skin.

### EPA
**EPA** — Eicosapentaenoic acid, an omega-3 fat found in fish and algae.

### 2-AG
**2-AG** — 2-arachidonoylglycerol, an endocannabinoid made from arachidonic acid that stimulates appetite through CB1.

### CB1
**CB1** — Cannabinoid receptor type 1, the receptor behind cannabis-induced appetite.

### EE
**EE** — Energy expenditure (calories burned).

### RMR
**RMR** — Resting metabolic rate.

### OR
**OR** — Odds ratio: the odds of an outcome in one group divided by the odds in another. The only measure a case-control study can give; approximates the RR for rare outcomes.

### RR
**RR** — Relative risk (risk ratio): the risk of an outcome in one group divided by the risk in another. See [[Observational study designs]].

### Anisogamy
**Anisogamy** — When an organism's gametes differ in size. In two-sexed species the small gamete (sperm) defines males and the large gamete (egg) defines females. See [[Biological sex]].

### Isogamy
**Isogamy** — When all of an organism's gametes are the same size, so there are no males or females. The ancestral state before anisogamy evolved.

### HR
**HR** — Hazard ratio: the rate of an event over time in one group divided by the rate in another. An HR of 1.71 means a 71% higher rate.

## Related concepts
- [[Proximal-to-distal sequence]]

### QCA
**QCA** — Quantitative coronary angiography: measuring coronary narrowings on X-ray angiograms with software, as percent diameter stenosis and minimum lumen diameter. See [[Coronary plaque regression]].

### MLD
**MLD** — Minimum lumen diameter: the width of the open channel of an artery at its narrowest point.

### NHL
**NHL** — Non-Hodgkin lymphoma: a group of cancers of lymphocytes, the white blood cells of the lymph system.

### AML
**AML** — Acute myeloid leukemia: a fast-growing cancer of the blood-forming cells in the bone marrow.

### AUC
**AUC** — Area under the curve: the area under a plot of blood concentration over time, a measure of total exposure to a substance after a dose.
