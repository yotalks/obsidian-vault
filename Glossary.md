## Abbreviations

### 1RM
**1RM / RM** — One-repetition maximum / repetition maximum — the heaviest load you can lift for that many reps.

### AAS
**AAS** — Anabolic-androgenic steroids. See [[Anabolic Androgenic Steroids]].

### BFR
**BFR** — Blood flow restriction (training with a cuff that limits blood flow).

### CNS
**CNS** — Central nervous system (brain and spinal cord). See [[Supraspinal CNS fatigue]].

### CSA
**CSA** — Cross-sectional area (of a muscle or muscle fiber).

### DOMS
**DOMS** — Delayed-onset muscle soreness.

### ECCF
**ECCF** — Excitation-contraction coupling failure — a calcium ion-related fatigue mechanism. See [[Calcium ion-related fatigue]].

### EMAL
**EMAL** — External moment arm length — distance from the load's line of force to the joint. See [[Internal moment arm]].

### IMAL
**IMAL** — Internal moment arm length — distance from a muscle's line of pull to the joint. See [[Internal moment arm]].

### MPS
**MPS** — Muscle protein synthesis.

### MPB
**MPB** — Muscle protein breakdown.

### MyoPS
**MyoPS** — Myofibrillar protein synthesis (the contractile-protein part of MPS).

### MT
**MT** — Mechanical tension. See [[Mechanical tension]].

### MUR
**MUR** — Motor unit recruitment. See [[Increasing motor unit recruitment]].

### RBE
**RBE** — Repeated bout effect. See [[Repeated bout effect]].

### GLP-1RA
**GLP-1RA** — GLP-1 receptor agonist (semaglutide, tirzepatide, retatrutide…). See [[GLP 1s]].

### GIP
**GIP** — Glucose-dependent insulinotropic polypeptide (also called gastric inhibitory polypeptide); GIPR is its receptor. See [[GLP 1s]].

### MACE
**MACE** — Major adverse cardiovascular events.

### TRT
**TRT** — Testosterone replacement therapy.

### TMAO
**TMAO** — Trimethylamine N-oxide, a gut-microbe metabolite of choline and carnitine. See [[TMAO]].

### LH
**LH / FSH** — Luteinizing hormone / follicle-stimulating hormone — pituitary hormones that drive the menstrual cycle. See [[Menstrual cycle]].

### hCG
**hCG** — Human chorionic gonadotropin, the pregnancy hormone measured in pregnancy tests.

### GHG
**GHG** — Greenhouse gas.

### GWAS
**GWAS / SNP** — Genome-wide association study / single-nucleotide polymorphism.

### SES
**SES** — Socioeconomic status.

### SMD
**SMD** — Standardized mean difference, an effect size used in meta-analysis.

### NAFLD
**NAFLD** — Non-alcoholic fatty liver disease: excess fat in the liver not caused by alcohol (now also called MASLD). See [[Lipogenic Fructose]].

### DNL
**DNL** — De novo lipogenesis: the liver making new fat from carbohydrate.
