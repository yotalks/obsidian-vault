The perpendicular distance from a muscle's line of pull to the joint's center of rotation (IMAL). A longer internal moment arm gives more joint torque for the same muscle force. The external moment arm (EMAL) is the same distance for the load; long EMALs create sticking points.

## Connections
**Feeds into**
- [[Sticking points in powerlifting]] — long external and shrinking internal moment arms create sticking regions
- [[Creatine Supplementation]] — a swollen muscle bulges further from the bone, lengthening its moment arm
- [[Running Economy]] — hypertrophy lengthens moment arms, so running needs less activation
