Stress hormones, mainly cortisol, released in response to stressors such as sleep loss and severe caloric restriction. In muscle they lower protein synthesis, raise protein breakdown and oxidative stress, and preferentially atrophy fast-twitch fibers. Anabolic steroids counteract them.
