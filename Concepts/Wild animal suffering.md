Most wild animals live short lives that end in predation, starvation, disease or exposure, because most species produce many offspring and nearly all of them die young. So nature contains enormous suffering. Several notes treat this as a premise: if wild lives are often bad, then protecting prey from predators, not breeding animals into the wild, and even displacing nature can be good.
