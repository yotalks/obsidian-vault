Peripheral fatigue caused by calcium ions accumulating inside muscle fibers. It covers excitation-contraction coupling failure (ECCF), reduced myofibrillar calcium sensitivity, and muscle damage. In these notes it underlies post-workout fatigue, the repeated bout effect, and shifts toward slower fiber types.
