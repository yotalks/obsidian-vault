Joel Salatin's Polyface Farm is often held up as a model for raising livestock, with claims that the animals move around, live like they're in the wild, and eat only natural things.
[Polyface video](https://youtu.be/sWYa53Z_NUY?t=305)

Visitors have documented that Polyface's pigs are fed grain grown on other farms: a standard corn and soy feed mix bought off the farm. They spend most of their lives in small pens on dirt, and even when on "pasture" they have free-choice feed at all times.
[Ebey Farm 2011](http://ebeyfarm.blogspot.com/2011/10/joel-salatin-and-his-pigs-pastured.html)

Salatin himself has said the animals are fed a lot of grain, and that he doesn't market the farm as "sustainable".
[Say What Michael Pollan 2011](https://saywhatmichaelpollan.wordpress.com/2011/02/11/the-grain-inputs-at-polyface-farm-joel-salatins-take/)
