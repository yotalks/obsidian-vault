> [!summary] Bottom line
> The common vaccine safety fears don't hold up. Infants get far more aluminum from breast milk or formula than from vaccines, and thimerosal (ethylmercury) clears quickly. Today's whole childhood schedule has fewer immune components than the single smallpox vaccine did, and more vaccines aren't linked to more infections. Large studies find no link to autism, type 1 diabetes, multiple sclerosis or autoimmune disease. The real but rare risks include anaphylaxis (about 1 per million doses) and a possible small rise in Guillain-Barré syndrome after flu vaccines, which is smaller than the risk from flu itself.

A review of the evidence behind common vaccine safety concerns.
[Geoghegan 2020](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7090020/)

The review declares no commercial or financial conflicts. One author, Paul Offit, co-invented the RotaTeq rotavirus vaccine.

## Ingredients

**Aluminum** is used as an adjuvant in vaccines such as hepatitis A and B, Hib and pneumococcal vaccines. A pharmacokinetic model found that the aluminum infants absorb from vaccines in the first months is less than what they get from breast milk or formula, and both stay below the regulatory minimal risk level.
[Mitkus 2011](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22001122/)

In infants, blood and hair aluminum levels weren't linked to vaccine history or to early development.
[Karwowski 2018](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28919482/)

**Thimerosal** is an ethylmercury preservative once used in multidose vials. Unlike methylmercury from fish, ethylmercury clears quickly and doesn't build up. Prenatal and infant thimerosal exposure wasn't linked to autism, and children given thimerosal-containing vaccines as infants didn't have worse neuropsychological performance 10 years later.
[Price 2010](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20837594/)
[Tozzi 2009](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19171612/)

**Human DNA** fragments remain in some vaccines grown in human embryo cell lines (varicella, rubella, hepatitis A and one rabies vaccine). The DNA is highly fragmented and can't readily enter the genome, and primate and modeling studies put any cancer risk at doses millions to trillions of times higher than in a vaccine.

## Number of vaccines

The smallpox vaccine alone contained about 200 proteins, while the 14 vaccines on the current US schedule contain about 160 immune components in total.

In 805,206 Danish children, more vaccinations weren't linked to more hospital admissions for infections the vaccines don't target.
[Hviid 2005](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16091572/)

In a US case-control study of 944 children, those hospitalized for nontargeted infections hadn't received more vaccine antigens by 23 months than those who weren't.
[Glanz 2018](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29509866/)

The number of antigens received in early childhood wasn't linked to neuropsychological outcomes at ages 7–10, and getting vaccines on time in the first year didn't harm neuropsychological outcomes compared with delaying them.
[Iqbal 2013](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23847024/)
[Smith 2010](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20498176/)

## Autism

The MMR-autism claim came from a 1998 case series of 12 children, which was later retracted for misrepresented data. The vaccination drop it caused in the UK was followed by a return of measles.
[Wakefield 1998 (retracted)](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/9500320/)

In about 500 children with autism in North London, the diagnosis was no more common or earlier after MMR introduction.
[Taylor 1999](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/10376617/)

In more than 500,000 Danish children, autism wasn't linked to MMR vaccination, the age it was given, or the time since vaccination.
[Madsen 2002](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/12421889/)

## Autoimmune and neurological conditions

- **Type 1 diabetes:** early vaccinations weren't linked to islet autoimmunity in more than 1,900 German children at high familial risk, and a meta-analysis of 23 studies (about 13,000 cases, 11 vaccines) found no link. [Beyerlein 2017](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28268073/) [Morgan 2016](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26564178/)
- **Multiple sclerosis:** a systematic review found no link with vaccination. [Mailand 2017](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27604618/)
- **HPV vaccine:** after almost 700,000 doses in Danish and Swedish girls, and in 134,615 vaccinated Finnish girls, autoimmune and neurological conditions weren't more common. [Arnheim-Dahlström 2013](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24108159/) [Skufca 2018](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30115524/)

## Real but rare risks

- **Guillain-Barré syndrome:** a meta-analysis of 39 studies found a relative risk of 1.41 after flu vaccines. But in Ontario, there were 1.03 admissions per million vaccinations, compared with 17.2 per million flu-related health care visits, so the flu itself carries more risk. [Martín Arias 2015](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25999283/) [Kwong 2013](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23810252/)
- **Anaphylaxis:** 1.31 cases per million doses, with no deaths. [McNeil 2016](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26452420/)
- **Oral polio vaccine:** vaccine-strain paralytic polio occurs in about 1 in 2.5 million recipients.
