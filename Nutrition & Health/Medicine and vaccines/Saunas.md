Saunas can have unintended adverse effects, the most notable being on male fertility. In 10 men with normal sperm, regular Finnish sauna sessions over 3 months strongly impaired sperm count and motility, as well as sperm DNA packaging and mitochondrial function, without changing sex hormones. The effects were completely reversed 6 months after stopping.
[Garolla 2013](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23411620/)
