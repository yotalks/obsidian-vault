> [!summary] Bottom line
> Finasteride slows male pattern hair loss and regrows some hair, and its sexual side effects affected under 4% of men in trials, versus 2% on placebo, mostly resolving. Oral 5-alpha reductase inhibitors work better than oral minoxidil, which has fewer side effects. On steroids, 5-alpha reductase inhibitors only matter for steroids that convert to something stronger, and with nandrolone they could make things worse. Creatine doesn't seem to cause hair loss.

## Finasteride

Male pattern hair loss (androgenetic alopecia) comes from androgen-driven miniaturization of scalp follicles, with scalp DHT a contributing cause. Finasteride blocks type II 5-alpha reductase, lowering DHT. In two 1-year trials of 1,553 men aged 18–41, finasteride 1 mg a day added 107 hairs at 1 year and 138 at 2 years in a 1-inch circle of the balding crown (from a baseline of 876), while men on placebo kept losing hair.
[Kaufman 1998](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/9777765/)

The study was run by Merck, which makes finasteride (Propecia).

### Sexual side effects

In the trials, 3.8% of men on finasteride reported decreased libido, erectile dysfunction or ejaculation disorders, compared with 2.1% on placebo. These resolved in men who stopped finasteride and in most of those who kept taking it, and each fell to 0.3% or less by the fifth year. After approval, there have been voluntary reports of sexual dysfunction continuing after stopping, and of depression and suicidal ideation, though reports like these can't establish how often they happen or whether finasteride caused them.
[FDA label: Propecia](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/lookup.cfm?setid=6f904709-65aa-44ce-b144-b4c8a0416e36)

### Compared with minoxidil

In a network meta-analysis of monotherapies for male pattern hair loss, oral 5-alpha reductase inhibitors were more effective than oral minoxidil, and dutasteride 0.5 mg a day ranked best for 6-month changes in total and terminal hair density.
[Gupta 2024](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38725143/)

So finasteride is more effective, while minoxidil has fewer side effects → [[Minoxidil]].

## Anabolic steroids

Starting prevention before a cycle works better than trying to regrow hair afterward.

5-alpha reductase inhibitors like finasteride and dutasteride only help against steroids that 5-alpha reductase converts into a stronger androgen, the way it turns testosterone into DHT. Nandrolone works the other way: in androgenic tissue it's converted into 5α-dihydro-19-nortestosterone, which binds the androgen receptor more weakly than nandrolone itself. Blocking that conversion would leave more of the stronger parent steroid in the scalp, so a 5-alpha reductase inhibitor could in principle speed up hair loss on nandrolone.
[Kicman 2008](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/18500378/)

## Creatine

The idea that creatine causes hair loss traces back to a crossover trial in 20 rugby players, where a week of creatine loading (25 g a day) raised DHT by 56%, and it stayed 40% above baseline after 2 more weeks at 5 g a day. Hair wasn't measured.
[van der Merwe 2009](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19741313/)

In a 12-week randomized trial of 45 resistance-trained men (38 finished), 5 g of creatine a day didn't change DHT, the DHT-to-testosterone ratio, or hair density, follicular unit count or hair thickness compared with placebo. Several authors advise or consult for supplement and creatine companies.
[Lak 2025](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40265319/)

Since DHT didn't change either, the trial is also evidence against creatine speeding up hair loss in men already prone to it.
