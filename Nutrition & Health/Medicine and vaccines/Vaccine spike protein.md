> [!summary] Bottom line
> The spike protein from SARS-CoV-2 can damage blood vessel cells in lab and animal studies, but vaccine-made spike differs in ways that matter. Most COVID-19 vaccines lock it in a shape that binds ACE2 less, it stays anchored to cells near the injection site, and only tiny amounts reach the blood, about 60–70 pg/mL, briefly. Large randomized trials, which capture whatever harm the spike might do, found no excess of serious harm.

## The toxicity finding

In hamsters, injecting a pseudovirus coated with spike protein damaged blood vessel lining, and in cell studies the damage depended on spike binding ACE2. The doses were high, and the study used only 3 animals per experiment. The authors noted that antibodies made in response to vaccination could block this spike-driven damage.
[Lei 2021](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33784827/)
[Science Feedback 2021](https://science.feedback.org/review/byram-bridles-claim-that-covid-19-vaccines-are-toxic-fails-to-account-for-key-differences-between-the-spike-protein-produced-during-infection-and-vaccination-misrepresents-studies/)

## How vaccine spike differs

**Shape.** In the Pfizer, Moderna, Johnson & Johnson and Novavax vaccines, two proline mutations hold the spike in its prefusion shape rather than the shape it takes when binding ACE2, which should reduce the binding behind the damage seen in these studies. This matters most for Novavax, which injects the spike protein itself. The Oxford–AstraZeneca vaccine makes unmodified spike. The prolines were added to improve the immune response, but whatever the reason, the vaccine spike lacks a step in the mechanism shown to cause damage.
[Lowe 2021](https://www.science.org/content/blog-post/spike-protein-behavior)

**Location.** The vaccines are injected into muscle, not a vein. Cells that take up the vaccine make spike that stays anchored in their membrane, on muscle and lymph node cells near the injection site, rather than spreading through the blood the way virus particles do in an infection. Most of the vaccine that does reach the blood ends up in the liver, and no other tissue in the animal studies got much more than 1% of the dose.
[Lowe 2021](https://www.science.org/content/blog-post/spike-protein-behavior)

**Amount.** In 13 health care workers given the Moderna vaccine, the S1 subunit of spike was detectable in the blood of 11 after the first dose. It peaked about 5 days later at an average of 68 pg/mL and was gone by day 14. Whole spike appeared in only 3, at an average peak of 62 pg/mL. Neither was detectable after the second dose. The antigen disappeared as antibodies against it rose.
[Ogata 2021](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34015087/)

## Outcomes over mechanisms

Whatever the spike does in the body, its effects show up in outcomes. The randomized trials of tens of thousands of people found serious adverse events and deaths at similar rates with vaccine and placebo ([[COVID-19 vaccine trials]]).
