> [!summary] Bottom line
> GLP-1 drugs (semaglutide < tirzepatide < retatrutide) produce large weight loss that is mostly fat, with muscle loss comparable to other weight loss and better physical function. Serious side effects are rare, GI effects fade and are reduced by slow titration, and weight returns after stopping unless maintained.

## Usage and efficacy

In early 2024, the Kaiser Family Foundation polled Americans about their GLP-1 drug usage and found 1/8 have used the drugs at least once.
[KFF poll 2024](https://www.kff.org/health-costs/kff-health-tracking-poll-may-2024-the-publics-use-and-views-of-glp-1-drugs/)

Ozempic creates great weight loss. Compared to lifestyle intervention and a placebo injection, a lifestyle intervention plus Ozempic led to a 15 percent reduction in body weight in this trial, with other trials showing similar results.
[Wilding 2021](https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa2032183)

Tirzepatide (Mounjaro, Zepbound) produces even more weight loss and greater health benefits.
[Jastreboff 2022](https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa2206038)
[Observatoire de la prévention 2023](https://observatoireprevention.org/en/2023/03/02/ozempic-semaglutide-and-mounjaro-tirzepatide-a-major-breakthrough-for-the-treatment-of-obesity/)

Compared head to head using propensity score matching, Tirzepatide consistently beats Ozempic.
[Rodriguez 2024](https://jamanetwork.com/journals/jamainternalmedicine/fullarticle/2821080)

Tirzepatide has a similar GLP-1 receptor agonist-based method of action, but it also adds [[Glossary#GIP|GIP]], or gastric inhibitory polypeptide, a weak inhibitor of gastric acid secretion and another insulin secretion stimulator. This makes Tirzepatide more effective, but a priori adds odds for side effects.

Retatrutide is even better as it contains those two ingredients plus glucagon, which creates interesting effects particularly in the liver. One phase 2 trial found that Reta has excellent effects on markers of liver health.
[Abdul-Rahman 2024](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0014299924007854)

Other trials confirm significant weight loss benefits. The weight loss impact seems larger than those for Tirz and Ozempic.
[Rosenstock 2023](https://www.thelancet.com/journals/lancet/article/PIIS0140-6736(23)01053-X/abstract)
[Jastreboff 2023](https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa2301972)
[Urva 2022](https://www.thelancet.com/journals/lancet/article/PIIS0140-6736(22)02033-5/abstract)

When mice injected with Reta or nothing and subjected to the same diets and temperatures, the Reta mice lost 35% of their body weight while the matched mice only lost 20%. When Reta mice were treated with glucagon antibody antagonists, this added weight loss effect stopped. Further research found that Reta increases fat mass reduction through increased lipid oxidation, supplementing energy levels, leading to maintained locomotor activity. This may also be related to why it seems to reduce visceral (belly) fat so selectively.
[Coskun 2022](https://www.cell.com/cell-metabolism/fulltext/S1550-4131(22)00312-6)

Trial results also found limited side effects with the drug.

## Retatrutide and heart rate

There does seem to be a cardiac safety signal which may be due to the glucagon, but it's not clear. This could potentially be important for people who have preexisting atrial fibrillation and CVD. 
Only one of these events became serious and the rest just passed: "Reported cardiac arrhythmias were mild to moderate in severity with the exception of one severe adverse event." The reason for this signal seems to be a dose-dependent increase in heart rate.
Increasing heart rate could help explain the added benefits of the drug for weight loss, but it's not clear as the heart rate increase was linearly dose-dependent, but weight loss capped out at 8mg.
For almost everyone, this isn't a problem, but those with a history of heart problems may want to consider using a [[Glossary#GLP-1RA|GLP-1RA]] without glucagon like semaglutide and tirz. These drugs reduce arrhythmia risk and cut the incidence of [[Glossary#MACE|MACEs]] immediately.
It seems like this is due to glucagon and not GLP-1R or GIPR agonism because trials with those drugs show reduced heart rates with weight loss (same as non-pharmacotherapy approaches) and a trial of the GLP-1RA/GCGN drug Mazdutide.
[Jastreboff 2023](https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa2301972)
[Eur Heart J 2024 abstract](https://academic.oup.com/eurheartj/article/45/Supplement_1/ehae666.442/7838497?login=false)
[Lincoff 2023](https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa2307563)
[Ji 2022](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9561728/)

## High-dose semaglutide

The efficacy evidence for high-dose semaglutide is poor. In a trial, it led to 18.7% weight loss vs. 19.5% and 20.9% for tirz at a 10mg or a 15mg weekly dose, and if everyone stayed on the drug, it's expected to have led to a 20.7% weight loss, as compared to 20.9% and 22% for the tirz doses. But high-dose semaglutide is rough with side effects, 25% of people assigned to 7.2 mg who stayed on had to step down to a lower dose, and side effects that are generally very rare for GLP-1RAs like tingling skin and severe GI issues were common with this dose.

Novo Nordisk wanted to try even higher doses to catch up to tirz which beats semaglutide on side effects and efficacy, and they barely managed to match them on efficacy while making their product unbearable for very large portions of the population and did so using a sample that was more female and fatter at baseline, which we expect to help it demonstrate greater efficacy. Though a benefit of these trials are that they provided more evidence that you can minimize most side effects through slow titration, without much loss of efficacy.
[Novo Nordisk press release 2025](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2025/06/20/3102978/0/en/Novo-Nordisk-Higher-dose-of-Wegovy-provided-average-weight-loss-of-21-in-people-with-obesity-with-a-third-achieving-25-or-more-according-to-data-presented-at-ADA.html)

## Side effects overview

Most of the popular side effects of GLP-1RAs (muscle wasting, pancreatitis, colorectal and thyroid cancer, major adverse cardiovascular events) are very rare and tied to pre-existing conditions (e.g. proliferative retinopathy); or are very exaggerated (e.g., GI sides). The most common sides are GI related and include things like nausea, vomiting, diarrhea, constipation, and delayed gastric emptying, but these sides are almost always non-severe, come and go fast, very population-specific, and rarer on tirz and reta than sema, and typically avoided with slow titration.

## Muscle and body composition

In each trial, people lose more fat than muscle, improving their body composition as they lose weight.

In this major trial on sema, the treatment group lost an average of 8.36 kg of fat and 5.26 kg of lean body mass. The placebo lost an average of 1.37 kg of fat and 1.83 kg of lean body mass. The relative contributions of fat to weight loss were 61.4% for the GLP-1RA group and 42.8% for the group losing weight with placebo
[Wilding 2021](https://www.nejm.org/doi/10.1056/NEJMoa2032183)

In a major Tirz trial, the treatment group lost 33.9% of their total fat mass and 10.9% of their lean body mass, vs 8.2% and 2.6% for the placebo group. Both outcomes were good, but the treated participants fared better: "The ratio of total fat mass to total lean mass decreased more with Tirz (from 0.93 at baseline to 0.70 at week 72) than with placebo (from 0.95 to 0.88)." 
[Jastreboff 2022](https://www.nejm.org/doi/10.1056/NEJMoa2206038)

Similarly, in a relatively short trial of Reta, participants saw considerable weight loss which was 2/3 fat and 1/3 lean.
[Retatrutide body composition trial (Obesity)](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1002/oby.23939)

At similar levels of total body weight loss, GLP-1RAs, and bariatric surgery produce clinically comparable and statistically indistinguishable reductions in lean body mass. 
[Neeland 2024](https://dom-pubs.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/dom.15728)
This has been pointed out in GLP-1RA trials
[Jastreboff 2022](https://www.nejm.org/doi/10.1056/NEJMoa2206038)

Lean mass is a good proxy for muscle, however, it is not just muscle, it also includes water, bone, and other tissues, in addition to fat infiltration. There is a new study that provides MRI measurements from the SURPASS-3 trial of Tirz and from the U.K. Biobank.
They looked at individuals in the large and popular U.K. Biobank cohort and assessed the relationship between non-pharmacological weight loss and muscle loss so that "normal" weight loss could be compared to the weight loss in a matched cohort that lost weight using GLP-1RAs. The relationships in either cohort were high and statistically indistinguishable.
[Sattar 2025](https://www.thelancet.com/journals/landia/article/PIIS2213-8587(25)00027-0/fulltext)

This alone, however, doesn't tell us enough about whether there was more or less muscle loss as it could be the case the intercepts differed. The amount of muscular volume lost at a given amount of weight loss has to be compared directly. The answer from that analysis was favorable, those on Tirz lost 0.04 liters more muscular volume at the same level of weight loss, which is both clinically unimportant and statistically insignificant.

The evidence above indicates that muscular volume is maintained, and that in it of itself is very strong evidence that GLP-1RAs don't cause muscle dysfunction, but it's not 100% proof. In the comparison between Tirzepatide-based weight loss and weight loss in the U.K. Biobank, additional evidence came in the form of research on fat infiltration.

Fat infiltration is the condition where fat gets in and between muscle fibers. This happens to everyone in varying extents, and it's particularly marked with age. It is strongly related to reduced strength and increases seem to cause failty and weakness.
[Fredwall 2023](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/s13023-023-02641-5)

Importantly, reductions are accompanied with improved physical functioning. There is research on this based on individuals who have received Roux-en-Y gastric bypasses. Their operations did cause weight loss (around 30%), did cause fat loss (around 48%), and did cause both lean mass loss (around 13%) and reductions in absolute strength (around 9%). However, relative strength increased by a much greater margin (32%) and this improvement came with significant improvements in physical performance measures, like improved walking speed and heightened physical activity levels.
[Alba 2019](https://academic.oup.com/jcem/article/104/3/711/5289722?login=false)

In individuals experiencing disease-related wasting and among those with metabolic disorders, fat infiltration tends to increase as weight declines.
[AASLD Liver Meeting 2023 abstract](https://journals.lww.com/hep/fulltext/10.1097/hep.0000000000000580~the-liver-meeting-boston-massachusetts-nov-10-14-2023#JCL2194)

Fat infiltration fails with GLP-1RA induced weight loss, as it does with non-pharmacological weight loss. This finding holds both compared to a control drug and compared to the weight loss in the U.K. Biobank sample. Therefore, we should have it as our prior that this sort of weight loss is not unlikely to improve rather than degrade muscular function.

There is no apparent excess reduction in metabolism after taking GLP-1RAs, indicating the weight loss is metabolically healthful.
[Blundell 2017](https://dom-pubs.pericles-prod.literatumonline.com/doi/10.1111/dom.12932)

Participants in trials report significantly fewer physical limitations in daily life and otherwise improved physical functioning and even quality of life.
[Wilding 2021](https://www.nejm.org/doi/10.1056/NEJMoa2032183)
[Rubino 2024](https://dom-pubs.pericles-prod.literatumonline.com/doi/10.1111/dom.15620)

## Pancreatitis

The evidence for pancreatitis as a potential side effect of GLP-1RAs is poor, it doesn't show more often in trials than in placebo groups and in high quality comparisons, risk isn't notably elevated. Risk may even be reduced.
There's generally bupkes for pancreatic cancer risk and evidence for increased pancreatitis risk is similarly poor.
Even with high doses of sema, they failed to see any elevation in pancreatitis risk.
In the SURMOUNT-1 study, each group taking Tirz had 1 case of pancreatitis, same with placebo.
Similar results were found in the SURMOUNT obesity trials.
Looking at this meta-analytically, there's no risk elevation for pancreatitis for Tirz in tirials at all, which includes trials with an without type-2 diabetics.
The risk is also similar for Sema compared head-to-head.
If there's elevated pancreatitis risk, it's at a low rate and we can't discount the need to ensure the comparison groups for demonstrating this risk are adequate because comparing diabetics to non diabetics wouldn't work.
[Ayoub 2025](https://www.mdpi.com/2077-0383/14/3/944)
[Jastreboff 2022](https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa2206038)
[Lilly Medical: pancreatitis FAQ](https://medical.lilly.com/us/products/answers/has-pancreatitis-been-reported-with-zepbound-tirzepatide-209516)
[Kamrul‐Hasan 2024](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1002/osp4.70032)
[Frías 2021](https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa2107519)

## Retinopathy

Diabetics who get their blood sugar under control fast have been found to be at increased risk of early worsening of diabetic retinopathy. These patients underwent intensive insulin treatment and diabetic retinopathy followed at pretty high rates. The mechanism is unclear.
GLP-1RAs do result in glycemic control coming about very fast, so, like insulin, they're likely to be a risk for those at risk.
A recent study looked at this with Tirz and found that there was no elevation in risk for retinopathy progression or maculopathy, but there was an increase in the risk of proliferative retinopathy. But, there was also a reduced risk of developing any retinopathy with those who didn't have it already.
These results were found in a sample with type-2 diabetes, but among the people in that sample, risk still differed by baseline retinopathy status. For type-2 diabetics without issues at baseline, and very likely for individuals who are also fine at baseline but not diabetic. It's likely that Tirz is protective, but for type-2 diabetics who don't know their risk, they might end up hurt by the drugs so they should visit an ophthalmologist before starting.
[Pappada 2018](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/s13104-018-3656-3)
[Akil 2021](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s13300-021-01190-z)
[Buckley 2025](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00125-025-06466-8)

## GI side effects

Tirz delivers less nausea and less severe nausea.
With GI outcomes, it's more ambiguous, but for the controllable ones that are usually the fault of the patient (N/V), the results definitely favor Tirz over Sema.
They favor Tirz in counted terms, but not nearly as much in terms of prevalence of any side, in fact there might be a downside, too, although very minor.
We do see reduced desensitization so the drug works for longer, both with benefits and sides. So while they tend to be more mild and maybe less common, they last longer.
[Knop 2024](https://dom-pubs.pericles-prod.literatumonline.com/doi/10.1111/dom.15875)

This study said "The most common adverse events in both treatment groups were gastrointestinal, and most were mild to moderate in severity and occurred primarily during dose escalation"
[Aronne 2025](https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa2416394)

With trials and pre-clinical studies, gastroparesis tends to go fairly quickly. A study found there was no medically-significant difference in gastric emptying for people in the placebo or Sema groups. The minimal differences may even be attributable to bodyweight since the groups people were in were assigned randomly, and it makes sense to adjust this for bodyweight given its theoretical relevance.
The same finding shows up in animals, in the first administration, rats not on GLP-1RAs, rather liraglutide, had far slower gastric emptying, but after 14 days of use, it becomes indistinguishable from placebo.
[Friedrichsen 2021](https://dom-pubs.pericles-prod.literatumonline.com/doi/10.1111/dom.14280)
[Jelsing 2012](https://dom-pubs.pericles-prod.literatumonline.com/doi/10.1111/j.1463-1326.2012.01557.x)

Here we see in this head to head trial comparing sema to tirz, sides fade out.
[Aronne 2025](https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa2416394)

We also expect diabetics have reduced rates of gallstones and ileus constipation on GLP-1RAs.
[Epic Research: GLP-1 GI effects in diabetics](https://www.epicresearch.org/articles/delayed-gastric-emptying-more-likely-gallstones-and-ileus-less-likely-for-diabetic-patients-on-glp-1-medications-compared-to-diabetics-on-other-treatments/)

With people who are merely obese, GLP-1RAs are associated with slightly increased rates of both conditions.
[Epic Research: GLP-1 GI effects in non-diabetics](https://www.epicresearch.org/articles/non-diabetic-patients-on-glp-1-medications-have-an-increased-likelihood-of-gi-side-effects-compared-to-patients-on-other-weight-loss-medications/)

Newer drugs tend to come with fewer sides as we see with rates of nausea and vomiting in comparable trials for Triz and Sema.
[Jung 2022](https://www.jomes.org/journal/view.html?doi=10.7570/jomes22012)

Tirz is better and safer than Sema because of its 5x weaker GLP-1R agonism that seems to happen due to co-occuring GIPR signaling from the hindbrain.
[Sun 2022](https://www.pnas.org/doi/10.1073/pnas.2116506119)

## Stopping and maintenance

After GLP-1RA discontinuation, the average regain at a year was 27.3%, for patients who didn't begin taking phentermine after. Those who did take it, kept much more off. It is a cheap anti-obesity medicine that's less effective.
[Epic Research: switching to phentermine](https://web.archive.org/web/20240608023003/https://www.epicresearch.org/articles/patients-who-lose-less-than-20-pounds-on-glp-1-treatment-lose-more-weight-after-switching-to-phentermine)

You can also just stay on it long-term, which eventually leads to a consistent weight as long as you stay on.
[Aronne 2024](https://jamanetwork.com/journals/jama/fullarticle/2812936)
[Bailony 2025](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1002/osp4.70069)

You can also switch to dosing every 2 weeks although they found 75% of the level from weekly usage.
[Cengiz 2025](https://dom-pubs.pericles-prod.literatumonline.com/doi/10.1111/dom.16229)

Thirty people given this dosing regiment saw maintenance after weight loss.
[Medscape 2025: every-2-week dosing](https://web.archive.org/web/20251118074607/https://www.medscape.com/viewarticle/de-escalating-glp-1s-every-2-weeks-maintenance-option-2025a1000ug4)

In this new trial after over a year of Tirz use, those that continued use maintained weight, using less maintained weight at a lower rate, and those that stopped also considerably maintained weight, with a lot of variance though because 2/3 received rescue shots. But with those intermittent shots, they kept about half of the lost weight.
[Horn 2026](https://www.thelancet.com/journals/lancet/article/PIIS0140-6736(26)00656-2/abstract)

This trial found maintenance was possible after Tirz or Sema by switching to once daily oral GLP-1RA orfoglipron.
[Aronne 2026](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41591-026-04386-7)

In this study, they were put on a diet to lose weight then were given Akkermansia muciniphila supplements which is an intestinal microbial symbiont that supposedly helps with weight and insulin control and it seemed to help. Although it was marginally significant and the authors have conflicts of interest.
[Mount 2026](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41591-026-04394-7)

## Storage and shelf life

Tirz has a shelf life of 2 years in a fridge and the finished product may be stored unrefrigerated for up to 21 days at a temperature not above 30 degrees C.
[FDA Mounjaro approval letter](https://web.archive.org/web/20250211131506/https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/appletter/2022/215866Orig1s000ltr.pdf)

Novo Nordisk achieved the same results (they even showed over 12 months, for 36 total, and up to 28 days below 30 degrees C, and 6 months at 25 degrees C).
[EMA Wegovy assessment report](https://web.archive.org/web/20250214104027/https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/documents/assessment-report/wegovy-epar-public-assessment-report_en.pdf)
