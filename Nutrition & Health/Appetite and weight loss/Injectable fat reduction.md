> [!summary] Bottom line
> CBL-514 is an injectable small-molecule drug that kills fat cells by apoptosis (programmed cell death) where it's injected. In company-funded phase 2 trials it removed about 200 mL of abdominal fat on average while body weight stayed stable, with mostly mild to moderate injection site reactions and none of the necrosis or nerve injury seen with Kybella, the only FDA-approved fat-dissolving injection. The trials were small, short and single-blind, and the cellulite and post-GLP-1 rebound results are so far only company press releases.

## Kybella

Deoxycholic acid injections (brand name Kybella, also called ATX-101) are the only FDA-approved injectable for fat reduction, and only for fat under the chin. Kybella is a cytolytic drug: it physically destroys the fat cell membrane, causing the cell to burst. In the pooled phase 3 trials (513 on Kybella, 506 on placebo), side effects at the injection site were common:
- swelling: 87% vs 43%
- pain: 70% vs 32%
- numbness: 66% vs 6%
- bruising: 72% vs 70%
- marginal mandibular nerve injury (an asymmetric smile or facial weakness): 4% vs <1%, all of which resolved without treatment
- trouble swallowing: 2% vs <1%

Injecting too superficially, into the skin rather than the fat, can cause skin ulceration and necrosis.
[Kybella label (FDA)](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=fe431ed4-ea6f-4e99-b4bc-ec25ae7b8553)

Deoxycholic acid mainly affects fat cells, but it's relatively nonselective and can also lyse muscle and other nearby tissue.
[Goodman 2022](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9384315/)

## CBL-514

CBL-514 is a small-molecule injection that kills fat cells by apoptosis instead of bursting them. In the company's cell and animal studies it inhibited the cell survival kinase DYRK1b, raised apoptosis mediators like caspases and the Bax/Bcl-2 ratio, and caused no microscopic damage or necrosis in organs and tissue around the injection site in rodents and dogs.
[Goodman 2022](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9384315/)

### Phase 2a

In an open-label phase 2a study of 43 adults, up to 4 treatments of CBL-514 (each 60 injections into abdominal fat) at the two higher doses significantly reduced abdominal fat volume and thickness. At the highest dose (2.0 mg/cm²), fat volume fell by about 120 mL, or 25%, in the per-protocol group. COVID-19 interrupted recruitment, and 8 participants withdrew because of adverse events like injection site pain, hives, rash or fever.
[Goodman 2022](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9384315/)

### Phase 2

In a randomized, placebo-controlled phase 2 trial, 76 adults (mostly white women, average BMI about 27–28) got up to 4 treatments of CBL-514 or saline, 4 weeks apart, into the abdomen. They had to keep their weight stable, and anyone whose weight changed by 3 kg or more was dropped. Average body weight barely changed in either group (+0.02 kg with CBL-514, −0.26 kg with placebo by the last follow-up).
- 8 weeks after the last treatment, 70% of the CBL-514 group had lost at least 150 mL of fat in the treated area, vs none on placebo, and 61% had lost at least 200 mL.
- Fat volume fell by about 210 mL on average (about 280 mL among those who completed the protocol), roughly 215 mL more than with placebo. The largest loss was over 750 mL.
- 43% of those who reached 150 mL got there after a single treatment.

In one 46-year-old woman with severe abdominal fat, fat thickness roughly halved (from about 3.9 cm to 1.9 cm), and 3D body scans showed the fat loss was concentrated where she was injected. The authors reported little or no loose skin after fat loss in the photographs.

Almost everyone had at least one injection site reaction, but this was also true with saline:

| Reaction      | CBL-514 (n = 49) | Placebo (n = 26) |
| ------------- | ---------------- | ---------------- |
| Pain          | 94%              | 65%              |
| Redness       | 94%              | 69%              |
| Itching       | 86%              | 42%              |
| Warmth        | 84%              | 31%              |
| Discoloration | 71%              | 12%              |
| Hardening     | 61%              | 8%               |

Pain was severe in 9 people on CBL-514, 60% of those with pain took medication (mostly over-the-counter painkillers), and pain resolved within a week in 91%. Two people stopped because of side effects. No necrosis, ulceration or nerve injury occurred. Two participants with high cholesterol had elevated blood lipids at one visit, one of which was judged probably related to the drug, and lab tests, vital signs and ECGs were otherwise normal.

Only participants were blinded; the investigators and sponsor knew who got the drug. About half of participants (49%) didn't complete the study, mostly from loss to follow-up, withdrawal and weight changes, and follow-up only lasted 8 weeks. The trial was funded by the drug's maker, Caliway Biopharmaceuticals, which helped design it, analyze the data and write the paper; three authors are Caliway employees and shareholders, and others are Caliway consultants or advisors.
[Gold 2025](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40037659/)

### Phase 2b

In a larger phase 2b trial of 108 adults with moderate to severe abdominal fat, given up to 4 treatments 3 weeks apart, 77% on CBL-514 improved by at least one grade on a clinician-rated abdominal fat scale, vs 19% on placebo. MRI showed abdominal fat volume fell about 150 mL, or 20%, from baseline. Side effects were mainly mild, short-lived injection site reactions. This trial was also single-blind and Caliway-run.
[Lorenc 2026](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42159040/)

### Compared to other fat removal

A systematic review of 25 studies (3,178 patients) found injectable fat-dissolving agents reduced local fat in almost all studies, with mostly mild, temporary side effects. But since they need multiple sessions, they cost more than a single liposuction session, though about the same as noninvasive options like fat freezing (cryolipolysis).
[Carrion 2026](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41296813/)

CBL-514's fat loss is in the range of fat freezing, where up to 24 cycles over 2 sessions removed about 265 mL from the abdomen and flanks in one study, though the measurement methods differed.
[Gold 2025](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40037659/)

## Other uses

**Dercum's disease** is a rare condition where people develop painful fatty lumps (lipomas) under the skin. In an open-label phase 2a study of 12 people with Dercum's disease, up to 2 CBL-514 treatments shrank lipomas by about 45–60% (median) at 4 and 8 weeks, and pain fell by 4 points on a 10-point scale. There was no comparison group, and the study was funded and partly written by Caliway.
[Herbst 2025](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12854054/)

The FDA gave CBL-514 orphan drug and fast track designations for Dercum's disease.
[Pharmacy Times 2024](https://www.pharmacytimes.com/view/fda-grants-orphan-drug-designation-for-cbl-514-to-treat-adult-patients-with-dercum-disease)
[Idris 2026](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42488923/)

**Cellulite:** Caliway reports that in a phase 2 study, over half of participants with moderate to severe cellulite improved by at least one grade 12 weeks after the last treatment.
[Caliway: CBL-514](https://www.caliwaybiopharma.com/en/research/15/)

**Weight regain after GLP-1 drugs:** Weight tends to come back after stopping [[GLP 1s|GLP-1 drugs]]. Caliway reports that in preclinical work, adding CBL-514 to a [[Glossary#GLP-1RA|GLP-1RA]] reduced weight and fat regain after the GLP-1RA was stopped. The idea is that killing fat cells, rather than just shrinking them, leaves fewer to refill. The species, design and numbers haven't been released.
[Caliway press release 2025](https://www.biospace.com/press-releases/caliway-to-present-first-preclinical-data-on-cbl-514-for-glp-1-weight-rebound-management-at-bio-2025)

## Conclusion

CBL-514 locally removes a few hundred milliliters of fat per treatment area without weight loss, and so far without the nerve injury, ulceration and necrosis seen with Kybella. Since it's a small molecule, it should be cheaper to make in bulk than biologic drugs. The evidence is still limited to small, short, single-blind phase 2 trials run by the company, so long-term safety, how long the results last, and the cellulite and GLP-1 regain uses still need independent phase 3 data.
