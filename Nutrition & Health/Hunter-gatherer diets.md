The Hadza of Tanzania are often cited as meat eaters, but they didn't eat mostly meat. Their diet falls into five main categories: tubers, berries, meat, baobab and honey. Honey was their most preferred food and tubers their least; tubers act as a fallback food, eaten more when berries are scarce. And what hunter-gatherers ate doesn't settle what's healthiest anyway.
[Marlowe 2009](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19350623/)
