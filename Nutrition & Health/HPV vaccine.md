> [!summary] Bottom line
> The HPV vaccine prevents 95–98% of precancers from the types it covers, and cervical cancer has already fallen in young women since it was introduced. Most Gardasil trials used an aluminum-containing placebo, but one trial in 9–15-year-olds used a saline placebo. Against saline, Gardasil caused more injection-site reactions but not more systemic side effects, new medical conditions or autoimmune conditions. Across all trials, autoimmune conditions were equally common with Gardasil and placebo. The polysorbate 80 in a dose is thousands of times below the levels that cause problems.

## Effectiveness

A review of common HPV vaccine myths summarizes the evidence.
[Bednarczyk 2019](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6746482/)

- **Precancer and warts:** in trials, efficacy against vaccine-type high-grade cervical lesions (CIN2+) was 95–98%, and against genital warts 89–98%.
- **Long-term:** in 2,084 vaccinated women followed for 12 years, there was one breakthrough low-grade lesion and no invasive cancer. [Kjaer 2018](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29029053/)
- **High-grade precancer:** in Tennessee, annual CIN2+ fell 24% in 18–20-year-olds and 10% in 21–24-year-olds in the vaccine era. [Oakley 2018](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29778314/)
- **Cervical cancer:** US rates in 2011–14 were 29% lower than in 2003–06 in women aged 15–24 and 13% lower at 25–34, with no change at 35 and older, who were mostly too old to be vaccinated. [Guo 2018](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29859731/)
- **Genital warts:** in Australia, the share of clinic visits for warts among women under 21 fell from 11.5% in 2007 to 0.9% in 2011, and among men under 21 from 12% to 2%, even though only girls were vaccinated. [Ali 2013](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23599298/)

About 90% of new HPV infections clear within two years, but so many people are infected that over a million US women aged 15–29 would still have persistent infections after two years. Pap smears only catch cervical lesions, not the vaginal, vulvar, anal, penile and throat cancers HPV also causes. Antibody levels are about twice as high when vaccinated before 15, which is why it's given at 11–12, and vaccination isn't linked to earlier or riskier sexual activity.
[Bednarczyk 2019](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6746482/)

## Safety against a saline placebo

Each Gardasil dose contains 225 µg of aluminum adjuvant and 50 µg of polysorbate 80. Most trials compared it with a placebo containing the same aluminum adjuvant, which can hide side effects caused by the aluminum. Protocol 018, in 9–15-year-olds, used a saline placebo, which the FDA reviewer singled out as particularly useful for that reason.
[[FDA Gardasil clinical review 2006.pdf|FDA clinical review 2006]]

In that trial, 1,781 boys and girls were randomized 2:1 to Gardasil or saline.
[Reisinger 2007](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17484215/)

**Injection-site reactions** were more common with Gardasil (75% vs 50%), and the aluminum placebo used in the other trials caused nearly as many as the vaccine did:

| Days 1–5 after any dose | Gardasil | Saline placebo | Aluminum placebo |
|---|---|---|---|
| Any injection-site reaction | 82.9% | 49.5% | 77.4% |
| Pain | 81.3% | 45.4% | 75.4% |
| Swelling | 24.2% | 7.7% | 15.8% |
| Redness | 23.6% | 13.2% | 18.4% |

**Systemic side effects** in the 15 days after any dose were similar (46.4% with Gardasil vs 44.5% with saline), with no significant differences: headache 19.0% vs 18.8%, fever 8.6% vs 7.7%, nausea 3.3% vs 3.8%, dizziness 2.1% vs 1.5%. Fever over 37.8 °C in the 5 days after a dose occurred in 7.2% vs 6.6%.

**New medical conditions** over the first 12 months were reported by 29.0% of Gardasil recipients and 31.0% of saline recipients, with similar rates for infections, musculoskeletal, neurological, immune and psychiatric conditions.

**Serious adverse events:** 5 occurred in the Gardasil group and none in the saline group: appendicitis, an infected toe, heavy uterine bleeding with anemia, kidney failure after finger surgery with several drugs, and type 1 diabetes diagnosed 4 days after the first dose in a boy whose blood glucose was already high on the day of vaccination. The reviewer judged none clearly related to the vaccine.
[[FDA Gardasil clinical review 2006.pdf|FDA clinical review 2006]]

**Long-term:** 8 years after vaccination, there were no new significant serious adverse events. None of the 429 children vaccinated at an average age of 12 developed persistent infection or disease from the vaccine types.
[Ferris 2014](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25136050/)

## Autoimmune conditions

Across the Gardasil trials, new conditions that could indicate a systemic autoimmune disorder were equally common with Gardasil and placebo (aluminum or saline): 2.3% vs 2.3% in girls and women (245 of 10,706 vs 218 of 9,412), and 1.5% vs 1.5% in boys and men (46 of 3,093 vs 34 of 2,303).
[Gardasil prescribing information](https://www.fda.gov/media/74350/download)

In the FDA's earlier pooled licensing data, specific autoimmune diagnoses (juvenile arthritis, rheumatoid arthritis, lupus) occurred in 3 of 11,813 Gardasil recipients and 1 of 9,701 placebo recipients, at rates similar to the general population. Several of the Gardasil cases already had joint symptoms before vaccination. Autoimmune thyroiditis occurred at 14.3 per 100,000, below the background rate of 30–150 per 100,000. The FDA still required autoimmune monitoring in a large post-marketing study.
[[FDA Gardasil clinical review 2006.pdf|FDA clinical review 2006]]

Later observational studies, covering up to 3.1 million women, found no consistent link with autoimmune disease. A few isolated signals may reflect vaccination visits leading to tests that uncover existing conditions. The only consistent safety signal is fainting just after the injection, which is why people are advised to sit or lie down for 15 minutes.
[Bednarczyk 2019](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6746482/)

## Polysorbate 80

Polysorbate 80 is an emulsifier used in over 3,000 medicines in Germany alone, and in foods, where the acceptable daily intake is 25 mg/kg of body weight. An EMA safety review set warning thresholds for injected polysorbates: 10 mg/kg as a bolus for cardiovascular effects, and 35 mg/kg a day for liver effects. The known liver harms came from premature infants given more than 80 mg/kg a day intravenously for days or weeks. Vaccines and protein drugs give under 0.25 mg/kg, and pharmacovigilance has shown no heart or liver signal after vaccines.
[EMA 2018](https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/documents/scientific-guideline/draft-information-package-leaflet-regarding-polysorbates-used-excipients-medicinal-products-human_en.pdf)

Gardasil's 50 µg is about 0.002 mg/kg for a 30 kg child.

At the high end, the chemotherapy drug docetaxel delivers up to 55 mg/kg of polysorbate 80 per dose, 3–4.5 mL in an hour-long infusion. Even then, blood levels at the end of the infusion were only about 0.1 µL/mL.
[Webster 1997](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/9118471/)

Polysorbate 80 isn't inert at those intravenous doses: it's linked to allergic reactions and infusion-site reactions with drugs like fosaprepitant. That review was funded by a company making a polysorbate-free alternative.
[Schwartzberg 2018](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6015121/)
