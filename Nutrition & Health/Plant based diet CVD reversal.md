A whole food plant based diet can prevent CVD from getting worse, reduce CVD events, make you healthier, reduce risk of heart attacks, strokes, dying of heart disease, the things that actually matter and go beyond CVD reversal, but not reversal.

In the Lifestyle Heart Trial, 28 patients with coronary heart disease went on a low-fat vegetarian diet, stopped smoking, and did stress management training and moderate exercise; 20 got usual care. After 1 year, average artery narrowing fell slightly in the lifestyle group (from 40.0% to 37.8% stenosis) and worsened in the control group (42.7% to 46.1%). That's a small regression in a small trial, and it came from several lifestyle changes at once, not diet alone.
[Ornish 1990](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/1973470/)
