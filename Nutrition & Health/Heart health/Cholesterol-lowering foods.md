> [!summary] Bottom line
> Tree nuts, avocados, oats, and plant proteins in place of red meat all lower LDL cholesterol in controlled trials. Nuts lower LDL-C by about 5 mg/dL per daily serving, with bigger drops at 60 g a day or more. An avocado a day lowered LDL-C more than a diet with the same fats from oil. The nut and avocado research was partly industry-linked.

## Tree nuts

A meta-analysis of 61 controlled trials (2,582 adults) found each daily 1-oz (28 g) serving of tree nuts lowered total cholesterol by 4.7 mg/dL, LDL-C by 4.8 mg/dL, [[Glossary#ApoB|ApoB]] by 3.7 mg/dL and triglycerides by 2.2 mg/dL. The effect grew more than proportionally at 60 g a day or more, and the type of nut didn't matter. ApoB fell more in people with type 2 diabetes.
[Del Gobbo 2015](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4658458/)
→ [[LDL and heart disease]]

The study was funded by the NIH. The organization that paid the authors to do it was separately paid by the International Tree Nut Council for a review of nuts, and one author has advised several food companies.

## Avocados

In a crossover feeding trial, 45 overweight or obese adults ate three cholesterol-lowering diets for 5 weeks each: lower fat, moderate fat with one avocado a day, and moderate fat with the same fatty acids from high-oleic oils. The avocado diet lowered LDL-C the most (13.5 mg/dL vs 8.3 and 7.4) and was the only one to lower LDL particle number and small, dense LDL.
[Wang 2015](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4330060/)

The trial was funded partly by the Hass Avocado Board, and one author sits on an avocado nutrition advisory board.

## Oats

In a crossover trial in 49 healthy adults, 6 weeks of daily oats lowered LDL-C from 125 to 117 mg/dL, while 2 eggs a day didn't change it.
[Katz 2005](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/15721501/)

## Plant protein instead of red meat

Compared with legumes, soy and nuts, red meat lowered LDL-C 0.20 mmol/L (8 mg/dL) less in a meta-analysis of 36 trials ([[Red meat]]).
[Guasch-Ferré 2019](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30958719/)
