> [!summary] Bottom line
> For healthy people without conditions like hypertension or kidney disease, usual salt intake is fine: mortality follows a U-shaped curve, lowest at usual intakes.

Healthy individuals without pre-existing comorbid conditions like [[Glossary#HTN|HTN]], [[Glossary#DM|DM]], [[Glossary#CHD|CHD]], CHD, Cirrhosis, [[Glossary#ESRD|ESRD]], etc, shouldn't need to care much about their daily salt intake. There's also evidence that the usual amount (2.6-4.9 grams of salt; 6.6-12.3 grams of NaCl) is actually associated with lower mortality than high or low salt intakes. Like most things, it's a U shaped curve.
[Graudal 2014](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24651634/)

Another study that found that it is a U shaped curve and most shouldn't worry about it.
[Graudal 2018](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0033062018300835)
