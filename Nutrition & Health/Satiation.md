> [!summary] Bottom line
> Fullness depends on stomach distention and slow gastric emptying: whole, moisture-rich, fibrous, viscous and high-protein foods beat liquids and dried foods.

## Gastric distention and emptying

Inflating stomach balloon reduced meal intake significantly which is key for early satiation during meal and robust satiety after.
[Geliebter 1988](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/3414573/)

Whole apples reduced total lunch calories more than applesauce or juice, matching weight and calories. Liquids drain stomach very rapidly.
[Flood-Obbagy 2009](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19110020/)

Water emptied faster than chicken liver, again showing liquids drain from stomach very rapidly.
[Fisher 1982](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/7083695/)

At equal calories, fresh mango produced some better appetite responses vs. dried mango. Dried foods are not good at delivering robust gastric distention without more calories.
[Stamper 2023](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10374964/)

Moisture rich foods where the liquid proportion is suspended in a complex matrix, like the cellular structures and fiber networks are better for gastric distention and gastric emptying.
[Aguilera 2019](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30040431/)

Fiber is great for gastric distention and delaying gastric emptying.
[Akhlaghi 2024](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36193993/)
→ [[Fiber]]

Adding oat β-glucan to an equal calorie, equal volume drink delayed proximal gastric emptying.
[Yu 2014](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24901089/)

Removing fiber shortened total emptying time from approximately 232 minutes to 186 minutes and hunger returned sooner.
[Benini 1995](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC1382616/?page=3)

## Protein

Higher protein shakes slowed gastric emptying in older adults.
[Dericioglu 2024](https://centaur.reading.ac.uk/120122/)

Equal calorie whey protein drink had half the emptying time when most of its protein was replaced with dextrose and olive oil.
[Bond University protein drink study](https://pure.bond.edu.au/ws/portalfiles/portal/29760074/Acute_Effects_of_Substitution_and_Addition_of_Carbohydrates_and_Fat_to_Protein.pdf)

Another review of protein being king for satiety and leading to weight loss because of it.
[Moon 2020](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7539343/)

## Other findings

Low carb diets tend to outperform low fat diets for weight loss.
[Nordmann 2006](https://jamanetwork.com/journals/jamainternalmedicine/fullarticle/409791)

Acid stable emulsions increases aqueous phase half-emptying time from 72 minutes to 171 minutes, increased CCK, and reduced one appetite measure.
[Marciani 2007](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17332474/)

Oatmeal created greater fullness, less hunger, slower gastric emptying, and lower intake for lunch over corn flakes.
[Geliebter 2015](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25612907/)

Increased meal viscosity slowed eating and gastric emptying and improved hunger and fullness ratings.
[Zhu 2013](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23818981/)

Whole food plant based diet induced more weight loss than whole food, animal based diet. Plant based likely had greater appetites as they consumed about 70 percent more grams of food total. The energy density was likely just not enough in plant foods to overcome greater appetite. Animal based had about 75 percent of calories from fat which is very calorie dense and likely outpaced their lower appetites. Fat is generally more satiating and sating than carbs, but also are very calorie dense.
[NIH news release 2021](https://www.nih.gov/news-events/news-releases/nih-study-compares-low-fat-plant-based-diet-low-carb-animal-based-diet)
