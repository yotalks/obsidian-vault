Nobody knows the optimal omega 6/3 ratio. When looking at primary prevention of heart disease like in the Cochrane review, there is no statistically significant improvement in supplementing with omega 3s even in American populations who already have high omega 6 to 3 ratio baseline. There is improvement in secondary prevention studies, but not primary.
