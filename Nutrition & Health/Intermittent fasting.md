> [!summary] Bottom line
> Intermittent fasting doesn't beat ordinary dieting for weight loss, and it may cost more lean mass.

In a 12-week trial of 116 adults with overweight or obesity, time-restricted eating (eating only between noon and 8 pm) led to no more weight loss than eating three meals a day. The time-restricted group also lost more appendicular lean mass.
[Lowe 2020](https://jamanetwork.com/journals/jamainternalmedicine/fullarticle/2771095)

A meta-analysis of 9 trials comparing intermittent with continuous dieting in people with overweight or obesity found no difference in weight, fat or other outcomes. The only significant difference was that regular intermittent dieting led to more lean mass loss (0.86 kg).
[Roman 2019](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41366-018-0204-0)
