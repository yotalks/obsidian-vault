> [!summary] Bottom line
> Strict long-term raw food diets are linked to large weight loss, underweight and lost periods, and the strictest followers are affected most. So they aren't a good long-term plan.

Raw food diets are associated with many stupid beliefs around self-purification like extensive fasting, not supplementing B12, and enemas.
[Koebnick 1999](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/10436305/)

As you approach 100% raw food, around half of them complain about amenorrhea, likely from insufficient calories. They also associate with quite low BMI scores.

In a survey of 513 long-term raw food dieters, men lost an average of 9.9 kg and women 12 kg after starting. BMI was under 18.5 in 14.7% of men and 25% of women, and it fell as the share of raw food rose. About 30% of women under 45 had partial to complete amenorrhea, and it was more common in those eating over 90% raw. This was a cross-sectional questionnaire, so it relies on self-report.
[Koebnick 1999](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/10436305/)
