> [!summary] Bottom line
> Farm animals get B12 supplements too. About 30% of B12 sales go to animal feed, so needing a B12 supplement isn't unique to vegan diets.

Around 30% of B12 sales are from the feed sector with the rest coming from food and pharmaceuticals. B12 supplementation happens to animals on a very large scale and it's likely a fair statement to suggest that this happens in the overwhelming majority of cases.
[Ullmann's Encyclopedia: Vitamins (2000)](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1002/14356007.a27_443)
