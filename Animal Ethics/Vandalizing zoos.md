The more pressure institutions that hold innocent animals captive have to spend their funds on things that are unrelated to animal care, the less money they will have to devote to caring for animals. Zoos will have to spend extra money on cleaning graffiti which will take funds away that could've gone to animal care and therefore increases the probability the innocent animals they keep are harmed.
