I view animal rights as the logical extension of human rights. I do not believe non-sentient human bodies, like brainless or brain-dead human bodies have human rights, so I therefore don't believe animals that lack brains have those rights either. Bivalves don't have brains and are not sentient, so I therefore see them as vegan to consume.
