> [!summary] Bottom line
> COVID vaccination can be compatible with veganism: manufacturing ingredients and animal testing are separate issues. Under the assumptions used here, the estimated human benefit greatly exceeds the animal-testing cost, including with sentience weighting. On utilitarian or threshold-deontological views, necessary animal testing can therefore be justified.

## Ingredients and manufacturing

According to the NHS, the COVID-19 vaccine (Pfizer) doesn't have any animal products.

Though a byproduct of bovine milk is used in the manufacturing process of the Pfizer vaccine (though not Moderna). It appears to be trace amounts, if this makes it not vegan, then it's not clear why potatoes wouldn't not vegan over eating legumes since they require more animal byproducts than legumes during the production of it.
[NHS: COVID-19 vaccine](https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/coronavirus-covid-19/coronavirus-vaccination/coronavirus-vaccine/)

According to the Vaccines and Related Biological Products Advisory Committee December 17, 2020 Meeting Presentation - Emergency Use Authorization Application for mRNA-1273 (Moderna Vaccine), the manufacturing process is cell free and has no human or animal products, preservatives, or adjuvants.
[FDA VRBPAC: Moderna EUA presentation (2020)](https://www.fda.gov/media/144583/download)

Despite having cholesterol, this is possible due to synthetic conversion of plant sterols into cholesterol. An example of a plant based cholesterol supplier for COVID vaccines is here.

## Animals used in testing

Estimation on the number of animals will use the highest number as a steelman. The estimation of the number of animals Pfizer used is:

Mice = 8 per group, times 4 groups = 32 mice.

Rhesus macaques = 6 per group (potentially an extra 3 non immunized matched monkeys for additional testing) = 21 macaques.

Total animals = 53

Assuming equal testing on BNT162b1, we get 64 mice, 42 macaques for a total of 106 animals.

## Estimated human lives saved

The Pfizer estimated capacity by end of 2021 is 1.3 billion doses and 650 million people vaccinated.
![[Pasted image 20261027200754.png]]

From the modified intention to treat cumulative incidence the ARR is about 2.2-0.3=1.9% per 112 days.
This yields a NNTV of 52.6
This means that with 650 million people vaccinated, 12,357,414 who will otherwise have gotten the diseases in the next 112 days will not get it.

The IFR is about 0.6%
![[Pasted image 20261027200930.png]]

This leads to 12,357,414 times 0.006 times 1.2 ≈ 88,973 lives saved per 112 days.
Assuming a linear relationship persists for the year, it translates into about 289,956 lives saved in 1 year: 1,449,780 in 5 years, 2,899,560 in 10 years, and so on. 

## Human lives saved per animal used

This leads to human lives saved over non-human animal per year being 2,735 in 1 year, 13,676 in 5 years, and 27,354 in 10 years.

## Sentience-weighted comparison

This also doesn't account for the sentience differential between non-human animals and humans. We can use cortical neuron count as a sentience correlate. 

14 million cortical neurons in mice times 64 mice = 896,000,000 cortical neurons
6.376x10^9 cortical neurons in macaques times 42 macaques = 267,792,000,000
8.6x10^10 cortical neurons in 1 human

(896,000,000+267,792,000,000)/(8.6×10^10) = 3.124 human eq cortical neurons

The estimate human sentience killed for this testing was ~3.124 humans. Using this estimate, per human killed, we get the following humans saved:
92,815 (1 year), 464,077 (5 years), 928,156 (10 years)

If one was vegan for utilitarian or threshold deontic views, it's very easy to make the case to animal test if it was necessary to release the vaccine.
