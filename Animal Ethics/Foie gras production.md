Foie gras is a luxury food made from the enlarged, fatty liver of ducks or geese. It's mainly produced by force-feeding: a tube is inserted down the bird's esophagus to deliver large amounts of feed, usually maize, at each feeding.

The EU's Scientific Committee on Animal Health and Animal Welfare found that ducks and geese show avoidance behavior before force-feeding, indicating aversion to the person feeding them and to the procedure. Shortly after being force-fed, birds that are able to move away from the person who fed them.
[SCAHAW 1998](https://food.ec.europa.eu/system/files/2020-12/sci-com_scah_out17_en.pdf)
