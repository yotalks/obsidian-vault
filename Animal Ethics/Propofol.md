> [!summary] Bottom line
> Propofol contains egg lecithin, but a typical procedure uses less than one egg's worth and sends very little money to animal agriculture. When no suitable alternative is available, the benefit of a medically necessary procedure very likely outweighs that cost.

## What propofol is

Propofol is a commonly used anesthetic agent used for induction and maintenance of general anesthesia as well as sedation, often preferred due its rapid onset and offset. Propofol is slightly soluble in water and thus is forumated in a white, oil-in-water emulsion. In addition to the active component, proposfol, the formulation also contains egg lecithin among other ingredients.

Given it contains egg, vegans may want to avoid receiving it during a procedure. While other agents are available for induction, maintenance and sedation, they may not be optimal or even contraindicated depending on the procedure and particulars of the patient.

## How much egg is used

How much egg is used? Propofol emulsion is 1.2% egg lecithin which is 12mg/mL.
[DailyMed: Propofol injectable emulsion](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/fda/fdaDrugXsl.cfm?setid=fdb77e13-f5a7-4c4a-9f8e-338b702239c8&type=display)

100g egg yolk = 5.9 egg yolk = 5-8 g lecithin
[MadSci Network: Lecithin in egg yolk](http://www.madsci.org/posts/archives/2005-03/1110595726.Bc.r.html)

More reliable source: 1 large egg yolk = 1.8g lecithin.
[NCT03842514 protocol](https://cdn.clinicaltrials.gov/large-docs/14/NCT03842514/Prot_SAP_000.pdf)

1% propofol = 10 mg/mL

### Dosing

Dosing:
Induction: 2-2.5 mg/kg IV for adults
Maintenance of anesthesia: 100-200 ug/kg/minute
Sedation: 40-100 ug/kg/minute

The label gives 25-75 ug/kg/minute for most patients during sedation for monitored anesthesia care, after starting at 100-150 ug/kg/minute for 3-5 minutes.
[DailyMed: Propofol injectable emulsion](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/fda/fdaDrugXsl.cfm?setid=fdb77e13-f5a7-4c4a-9f8e-338b702239c8&type=display)

## Cost to animal agriculture

An average 30 minute surgery using propofol won't even use 1 egg's worth of lecithin, it comes to around 0.4 eggs which given retail cost of around 1.4 dollars per egg in the USA, the net result is around 59 cents to animal agriculture.

The US average retail price of a dozen large eggs was $2.27 in September 2026, or about 19 cents per egg, which would make 0.4 eggs about 8 cents.
[FRED: Average price of eggs](https://fred.stlouisfed.org/series/APU0000708111)

## Ethics

Whatever deontic right violation by contributing 59 cents to animal agriculture is very likely outweighed by the consequences assuming this is a medically necessary procedure (when no medically suitable alternative is available).
