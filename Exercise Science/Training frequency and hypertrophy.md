> [!summary] Bottom line
> Research on frequency for hypertrophy is thin: most comparisons find no difference, with only a weak trend favoring 3× over 1× per week.

There are very few training studies when assessing training frequency for hypertrophy. The only trend is for 3x per week being better than 1x per week.

Study on trained adults found no differences between 1 and 2 times frequency.
[Brigatto 2019](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29528962/)

Study on untrained adults comparing 1 and 2 times frequency found no differences.
[Gentil 2015](https://www.researchgate.net/profile/Martim-Bottaro-2/publication/261735493_Effects_of_equal-volume_resistance_training_performed_one_or_two_times_a_week_in_upper_body_muscle_size_and_strength_of_untrained_young_men/links/54da487b0cf261ce15cc9045/Effects-of-equal-volume-resistance-training-performed-one-or-two-times-a-week-in-upper-body-muscle-size-and-strength-of-untrained-young-men.pdf)

Study comparing 1x and 2x frequency on trained adults found no differences.
[Tavares 2017](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28316261/)

Study comparing 1 and 3 times frequency in trained adults found no differences but 3x was numerically better.
[Ochi 2018](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30013480/)

Study on trained adults comparing 1 and 3 times frequency found 3x is better than 1x.
[Schoenfeld 2015b](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25932981/)

Study comparing 1 and 3 times frequency on trained adults found 3x is better than 1x
[Neves 2022](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36228016/)

Study on untrained adults comparing 2 and 3 times frequency found no differences.
[Barcelos 2018](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29852092/)

Study on untrained elderly comparing 2 and 3 times frequency found no differences
[Grzyb 2022](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32028459/)

Study on trained adults comparing 2 and 3 times frequency found no differences.
[Lasevicius 2019](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31531139/)
