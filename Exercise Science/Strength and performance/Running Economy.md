> [!summary] Bottom line
> Strength training can improve running economy by raising vertical stiffness through eccentric strength, and through hypertrophy lengthening internal moment arms so less activation is needed.

## Factors where strength training can improve running economy: 

### Vertical stiffness: 

When running, you have vertical oscillation, moving up and down of center of mass, greater stiffness means less of this oscillation, making you more efficient and you're able waste less energy for every footfall.

We can improve this vertical stiffness by increasing our eccentric strength in the muscle fibers used in the running activity.

This can be done with strength training that has highish activation on the eccentric phase (very high recruitment not really necessary as not recruiting those fibers on submaximal running). So we can do heavy strength training to train for this.

### Greater internal moment arm:

Hypertrophy of a muscle causes the muscle to bulge further from the bone, increasing the [[Internal moment arm|internal moment arm length]] to the joint center. This increased moment arm will give greater joint torque for a given muscle force which will increase the torque you'll be able to generate at lower levels of muscle activation which is beneficial because you're using less metabolic activity for same output. This will also provide benefit for cyclists.
