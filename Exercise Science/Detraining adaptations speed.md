> [!summary] Bottom line
> After training stops, sarcomeres are lost in about 2 weeks, hypertrophy in about a month, tendon stiffness in about 2 months, and neural adaptations over 6–12+ months. Small doses maintain them: roughly 3–4 sets once a week for size and 1–2 stretched-position sets a week for sarcomeres.

Detraining refers to the loss of adaptations after the cessation of a strength training program. Since each adaptation is produced by its own unique stimulus, each adaptation similar dissipates in response to the absence of specific conditions and at different rates. Usually after strength training programs finish, there are losses in muscle fiber cross sectional area, sarcomeres in series, tendon stiffness, and various neural adaptations.,

## How fast each adaptation is lost

After a standard strength training program (8-12 weeks), neural adaptations (coordination and increases in ability to recruit high threshold motor units), it takes 6-12 months or even longer to lose those adaptations.
[[Kubo 2010]]
→ [[Improvements in coordination traits]]

We see that tendon stiffness adaptations are lost in 2 months
[Shima 2002](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/11990741/)

Hypertrophy (increases ion muscle fiber [[Glossary#CSA|CSA]] though the addition of myofibrils in parallel) are lost in a month.
[Kadi 2004](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC1665027/#sec8)

[[Sarcomerogenesis|Sarcomerogenesis]] (an increase in sarcomeres in series) are lost in 2 weeks.
[[Sparrow 1982]]
[[Presland 2018]]
[Pollard 2019](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30629773/)
[Crawford 2025](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40085810/)
→ [[Detraining sarcomerogenesis]]

## Maintenance

This means short periods of detraining will mainly only involve the loss of sarcomeres in series and muscle fiber CSA. So, tapers, deloads, and other periods of reduced levels of training should focus on avoiding loss in muscle size. 

We see maintenance literature identifies the workout volume and frequency needed to keep existing levels of muscle fiber CSA and sarcomeres in series.

Studies have shown that hypertrophy is maintained with 3-4 sets once per week.
[Crawford 2025](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40085810/)
[[Trappe 2002]]
[Mpampoulis 2024](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11280775/)
[Rønnestad 2010](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20799042/)

We also have data that 1 set twice a week causes hypertrophy.

Sarcomerogenesis requires even less weekly stimulus to maintain. Maintenance of sarcomeres can be achieved with just 1-2 sets of stretched position exercises once per week.
[[Miura 2025]]
[[Behan 2022]]
