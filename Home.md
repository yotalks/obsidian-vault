## How the vault is organized
- Each topic folder has a MOC at its top level, and its notes sit in subfolders that follow the MOC's sections. For example, Exercise Science splits into Fatigue and recovery, Hypertrophy, Programming, Strength and performance, Injuries and tendons, Supplements and drugs, and Muscle training.
- **Concepts** holds ideas that several topics build on, and each one is defined in the [[Glossary]].
- **Sources** holds studies cited in several notes.
- **Attachments** holds images.
