All trans debates pretty much boil down to semantics about what constitutes a man or woman. If we define a man as someone who possesses a sufficiently greater sum of weighted traits that more closely associate with small gametes and a woman as someone who possesses a sufficiently greater sum of weighted traits that more closely associate with large gametes. And they are over the age of 18. A trans person that possesses those characteristics are that gender.
