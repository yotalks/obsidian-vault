FBI and DHS reports define domestic terrorism using 18 USC § 2331(5): acts dangerous to human life that break criminal law, appear intended to intimidate civilians or influence government, and occur mainly in the US. No federal crime is called domestic terrorism, so the label and the charges are separate questions.
