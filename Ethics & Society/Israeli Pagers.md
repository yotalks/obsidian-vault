Based on the definitions found in the IHL databases, a booby trap is a device that is designed, constructed, or adopted to kill or injure when a person disturbs or approaches an apparently safe act. The booby traps were set off remotely and not designed to set off merely on the execution of an apparently safe act. So the Israeli pagers weren't booby traps.
