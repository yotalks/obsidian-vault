> [!summary] Bottom line
> The US spends more on healthcare mainly because it's richer: richer countries consume more care, at higher intensity and with more technology. On this view, high US spending isn't mainly driven by wages, profits, administration or the number of doctor visits.

## Income explains spending

Rich countries consume much higher quantities of healthcare, through more intensive and higher-tech care. Spending isn't high and rising because of wages, profits, administration or the number of encounters, and national income explains American spending as well as it explains other countries'.
[Random Critical Analysis: Why conventional wisdom on health care is wrong](https://randomcriticalanalysis.com/why-conventional-wisdom-on-health-care-is-wrong-a-primer/)
## Spending vs. life expectancy

Higher spending hasn't bought longer life. Most of the US life expectancy gap comes from overdoses, firearms, car accidents and circulatory disease, while on screenable/treatable cancers the US actually does slightly better than its peers.
