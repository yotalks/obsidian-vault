The only historical examples of bigoted terms, like slurs, becoming not harmful to the people that were historically targeted by the terms, involve changing the usage of the term to something not bigoted. There is no historical precedent that has been as successful as this for achieving this goal. For example, "nigga" being used to mean "bro".

Retard is also almost never used to refer to people who are actually mentally disabled. This is different to other slurs which are frequently used to refer to the minority.
